# D4 training/ — label INPUTS (the crown jewels)

Hand-curated labels that drive model training and evaluation. These are **inputs**, not
regenerable outputs — treat them as the most valuable artifacts in D4. (Renamed/relocated here
from `output/` so inputs and outputs are cleanly separated.)

## Files

| file | trains | granularity | key columns |
|---|---|---|---|
| `classifier.csv` | the SetFit candidate classifier (`04`) | **candidate-level** (one row per date candidate) | `label` ∈ {initiation, decision, final_eis, neither}, `split` ∈ {train, test} |
| `ranker.csv` | the LightGBM ranker (`05b`) | **project-level** (one row per project) | `initiation_candidate_id`, `decision_candidate_id` (the true picks), `split` |
| `eis_validation/` | held-out yardstick | project-level, Codex-verified | `eis_{rod,feis}_*_sample_labeled.csv` (true ROD / FEIS dates) |
| `frozen_eval_ids.txt` | *(to be added)* the eval registry | — | project_ids reserved for evaluation, **never** trained on |
| `_backups/` | timestamped backups | — | gitignored |

The two files differ in **row shape** because they train different models (per-candidate label vs.
per-project pick) — but they share one **discipline**:

## Discipline (the guardrails)

1. **Frozen `split`.** Assigned once, stratified; `test` rows are never trained on and never
   re-randomized. New rows default to `train` so the test set can't grow or leak.
2. **A label is training XOR evaluation — never both.** The `eis_validation/` yardstick and the
   (forthcoming) `frozen_eval_ids.txt` must be excluded from training. Do not fold a yardstick
   label into `classifier.csv`/`ranker.csv` and then also evaluate against it.
3. **Inputs, not outputs.** Nothing here is regenerated by a pipeline run; edits are deliberate
   (re)labeling. Generated artifacts live in `output/deliverable04/`.

> Note: all `*.csv` here are gitignored by the repo-wide rule. Versioning the labels is a separate
> decision (they're ~6 MB each); until then they are local-only — back them up out of band.
