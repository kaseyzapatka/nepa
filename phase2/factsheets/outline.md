[Fact Sheet 4: Visual Impacts](https://www.kaseyzapatka.com/nepa/phase2/reports/deliverable03.html)

  - Brief executive summary/key findings

  - Introduction
    
      - Explanation of visual impact assessments in NEPA reviews (why they’re done, BLM’s VRM system)
    
      - Visual impacts often a concern for decarbonization technologies such as transmission and wind and fossil fuel technologies such as oil and gas

  - Methodology

  - Results
    
      - Word cloud
    
      - Framing analysis
    
      - Topic analysis and validation

  - Conclusion
    
      - Visual impacts concerns exist across technologies
    
      - Specific concerns and strength of concerns vary, with industrial and infrastructure corridors leading the list; shadow flicker a big concern for wind
    
      - Developers should take visual impacts concerns seriously and make efforts to mitigate them when possible
