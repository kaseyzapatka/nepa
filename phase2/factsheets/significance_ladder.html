Significant and unavoidable
The extreme case: the impact is significant and no available measure can bring it below the threshold.
Significant adverse
The impact crosses the significance threshold, so the full review applies.
THE SIGNIFICANCE LINE
Less than significant with committed mitigation
The impact would cross the line, but specific required measures pull it under — the mitigated FONSI. These sit right at the line and are this fact sheet's central subject.
Less than significant
A real but minor impact that stays under the line on its own, with no committed measures needed.
No significant impact
The agency finds no meaningful impact on the resource at all — the below-the-line default.
